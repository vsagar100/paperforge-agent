# Publication requirements

The supplied prompt is retained verbatim in MANUSCRIPT_REQUIREMENTS.md. Staged planning,
source appraisal, technical sections, paragraph evidence, IEEE assembly, review loops,
placeholders and QC implement its central requirements.

Do not invent experiments, citations, results, approvals or metadata. Missing accessible
detail is separate from a source's author-reported scientific limitation. Quote matching
is an auditable prerequisite, not proof that the exact claim is supported.

The workflow delivers an author-review packet. Table/figure integration, complex equation
typesetting, journal templates, native simulation execution and scientific image
interpretation still require further work. It never promises plagiarism clearance,
AI-detector evasion, publication acceptance, SCI/SCIE indexing or submission readiness.
